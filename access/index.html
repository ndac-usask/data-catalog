    Request Access

    How the process works

    NDAC operates a discovery-first data portal. You explore dataset descriptions here, then contact us if a dataset is relevant to your research or collaboration.

    Discover → Review → Inquire → NDAC Triage → Response → (Approval + Conditions)

    Step-by-step

    1. Discover a dataset Browse the dataset catalog and read the dataset page that interests you.

    2. Review the documentation Each dataset page includes a summary, resolution, temporal coverage, data types, and access level.

    3. Send an inquiry Email Thuan.ha@usask.ca with:

    What to include Why
    Dataset ID (e.g. NDAC-CROPMASK-PRAIRIES-F25T25) Identifies the exact dataset
    Your name and organisation Establishes who is requesting
    Purpose of the request Helps NDAC assess fit
    Intended use Required for restricted datasets
    Preferred timeline Helps prioritise responses
    Whether collaboration is of interest Opens additional paths

    4. NDAC reviews your request We will assess: dataset shareability, sensitivity, partner constraints, and whether a meeting is needed first.

    5. We respond Possible outcomes:

    • Share general information only
    • Request clarification or schedule a call
    • Approve access in principle (with conditions)
    • Defer due to restrictions or partner agreements

    6. If approved — conditions are documented Approved access includes: permitted files, permitted uses, attribution expectations, and any data-sharing agreement requirements.

    Access levels explained

    Badge Meaning
    Open Publicly available or openly licensed; source links provided where applicable
    Internal Available to NDAC team members; external access requires discussion
    Restricted Formal request required; sensitivity or partner constraints apply
    By Request Contact the NDAC data team to discuss access

    Response time

    We aim to respond to all inquiries within 5 business days. Complex requests involving restricted or partner data may take longer.
