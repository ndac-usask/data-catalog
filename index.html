    About NDAC

    The Nutrien Digital Agriculture Centre (NDAC) is part of the College of Agriculture and Bioresources at the University of Saskatchewan, whose main campus sits on Treaty 6 Territory and the Homeland of the Métis. The centre advances research, teaching, and innovation to accelerate farming into the digital age and to build more sustainable, resilient food systems — positioning Saskatchewan as a global leader in sustainable digital agriculture and USask as a hub for agricultural AI expertise.

    From Saskatchewan to the world: advancing the future of agriculture through bold ideas, collaborative discovery and digital innovation.

    Our team develops machine learning tools and datasets for precision agriculture and environmental monitoring across the Canadian Prairies — spanning remote sensing, UAV imagery, soil, crop yield, terrain, and interactive web applications.
