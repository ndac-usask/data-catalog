    Nutrien Digital Agriculture Centre · University of Saskatchewan

    What is this portal?

    The NDAC Data Discovery Portal lets researchers, collaborators, and partners explore the datasets held by the Nutrien Digital Agriculture Centre (NDAC) at the University of Saskatchewan.

    Discovery only — no automatic download

    This portal describes what data exists, its scope, and how it was created. Data files are not directly downloadable here. If a dataset is relevant to your work, use the Request Access page to start a conversation with the NDAC team.

    About NDAC

    The Nutrien Digital Agriculture Centre (NDAC) at the University of Saskatchewan develops data-driven tools for precision agriculture and environmental monitoring across the Canadian prairies.

    Our datasets cover Saskatchewan, Alberta, and Manitoba and span remote sensing, UAV imagery, soil sampling, crop yield, and wetland mapping.

    All data is managed under:

    • Tri-Agency Research Data Management Policy
    • OCAP® Principles (Ownership · Control · Access · Possession)
    • CARE Principles for Indigenous data governance
    • CRDCN standards
