    What to include in your message

    To help us respond efficiently, please include:

    • The dataset ID you are interested in (e.g. NDAC-YIELD-001)
    • Your name, role, and organisation
    • A brief description of your research purpose or intended use
    • Whether you are seeking a collaboration or a one-time data request

    About NDAC

    The Nutrien Digital Agriculture Centre (NDAC) is located at the University of Saskatchewan. We develop machine learning tools and datasets for precision agriculture and environmental monitoring across the Canadian prairies.

    Our data management practices follow:

    • Tri-Agency Research Data Management Policy
    • OCAP® Principles
    • CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance
    • CRDCN Standards
